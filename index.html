Getting to Jantar Mantar when the metro stations are shut

Ask how the 9 to 10 October 2026 closure of 45 Delhi metro stations changed a journey. Every answer comes from a graph of the Delhi Metro. The route and the queries are shown underneath.

    Closure data comes from DMRC's update of 9 October 2026 as reported by Business Today, and a Delhi Police proposal reported by News On AIR. Entry and exit were closed at 45 stations; DMRC said passengers could still change trains at all of them except New Delhi. Ride times come from the DMRC timetable on Delhi Open Transit Data (Department of Transport, GNCTD; 2023 version). This project is not endorsed by DMRC or the Department of Transport. Walking times are estimates from straight-line distance. Buses, taxis and other road transport are not modelled.
